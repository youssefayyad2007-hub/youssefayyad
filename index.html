Autonomous Domino Placing Robot

OnshapeC++ motion controlTeam of 4

Project 02

Geared Keychain Assembly

SolidWorksGD&TDFMIndividual project

Designed a five-part mechanical keychain in SolidWorks: a spur gear keyring connector free to rotate between two retaining plates, separated by a curved spacer and secured with socket-head fasteners.

Project 03

Rope Brake Dynamometer

TetrixMechanical testingTeam of 5

Built and instrumented a rope-brake dynamometer to characterize the torque-speed behaviour of a small DC motor, using an elastic element in place of a commercial load cell to keep the design simple and inexpensive to calibrate.

Calibrated the elastic's force response experimentally, then used that curve to convert rope-tension measurements into torque across a range of motor speeds, recovering the linear torque-speed relationship expected of a brushed DC motor. The measured stall torque and no-load speed matched the motor's rated performance within a few percent, confirming the rig held up as a low-cost stand-in for a commercial load cell.
